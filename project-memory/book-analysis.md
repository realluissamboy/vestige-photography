# Analysis: *Vestige — Twenty Years of Modern Pin-Up*

Susana's coffee-table monograph (Wonk Press, 2025, ISBN 979-8-9927849-0-9). 40-page preview reviewed 2026-04-17. Interior + cover design by Carrie A. Smith.

This is Susana's brand anchor. What's in this book is more authoritative than anything in the website spec files.

---

## Visual language

### Primary wordmark
- "Vestige" set as a **bold, flowing hand-written script** — a single signature-like mark. Used on cover, spine, and as a chapter-mark element throughout.
- Rendered in **bright crimson red** on a strong background (white, pink, or colored block).
- This is the book's signature visual. It is NOT the current site's Cormorant Garamond light italic — they're different moods entirely.

### Section titles
- "Modern [X]" — **bold italic serif**, crisp white, on a solid color block.
  - Modern Glamour, Modern Midcentury, Modern Kulture, Modern Tiki, etc.
- Stands alongside the script wordmark and secondary section labels (also script): "Contents," "Foreword," "Introduction."

### Color palette (section openers)
Each chapter opens on a full-bleed color block. Approximate values pulled from the book:

| Role | Approximate hex | Seen on |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Crimson / scarlet | #C8142C | Primary Vestige mark, accent blocks |
| Dusty rose / muted coral | #C47F7A | Foreword |
| Muted plum / mauve | #8B6F7C | Contents |
| Muted teal / sage-green | #5A9B98 | Modern Midcentury |
| Dusty blue | #5D7F9A | Modern Kulture |
| Deeper viridian teal | #317B73 | Modern Tiki |

Book is **confident color-forward**, not restrained. The photographs carry saturated color (reds, pinks, teals, oranges, greens, gold) and the design frames them to match, not to retreat.

### Layout patterns
- **Section openers:** 6-photo grid with a centered colored title tile (italic serif "Modern X" over a solid block).
- **Two-photo stacked landscapes** as breather spreads.
- **Full-bleed singles** for marquee images.
- Content sits comfortably on colored backgrounds — book treats color as a supporting character.

### Typographic feel
Script + bold italic serif is the core pairing. Warm, editorial, confident, unmistakably *pin-up monograph* rather than *fine-art gallery catalog*.

## Written voice

Sources: the cover copy, the Foreword (Angelique Noire), the Introduction (Heidi Van Horne). Susana's own longer writing isn't in the preview but the chosen co-writers set the voice of the collaboration.

### Tone
- **Warm, inclusive, community-driven.** "Pin-up world," "pin-up scene," "this movement thrives on collaboration."
- **Celebratory, not clinical.** "Lively, colorful celebration of individuality, confidence, and creativity."
- **Self-aware humor.** Heidi on herself: *"In the harsh light of the real world, I am hardly always a pin-up; instead, I am a nerd. A grease monkey. A goofball. Inelegant and obtuse."* — the voice doesn't take itself too seriously even when the craft is serious.
- **Personal / memoir-style** interwoven with cultural commentary. Reads like a letter from a friend with a thesis, not a corporate bio.

### Recurring themes
1. **"Modern" prefix.** Her entire taxonomy uses "Modern [X]" — Modern Pin-Up, Modern Boudoir, Modern Glamour, Modern Midcentury, Modern Kulture, Modern Tiki, Modern Self-Portraits. This is her positioning: the contemporary reclamation of a historic form.
2. **Inclusivity and diversity.** Explicit throughout — race, body type, identity, "and a few men too." A deliberate departure from classic pin-up's narrowness.
3. **Confidence / empowerment.** "Creating a full experience for her models that allows for an overall affirming encounter." "Embrace their confidence, femininity, and playfulness."
4. **The experience, not just the image.** Susana's "secret sauce" per Baby Doe's cover quote: the shoot is a full affirming experience, the image is the artifact.
5. **Craft lineage.** Hurrell, Vargas, Elvgren, Bernie Dexter, Sabina Kelley. Rooted in a specific photographic tradition she names.
6. **Community over hierarchy.** Models, stylists, hair, wardrobe, set, cocktails, cars — all named and credited. It's a scene, not a studio.

### Phrases worth echoing
- "Modern Pin-Up"
- "Timeless portraits that embody classic beauty"
- "Vintage charm with a modern flair"
- "Retro-inspired scenes"
- "A celebration of diversity... a richer and more authentic tapestry"
- "Modern Kulture" (note the *K* — subculture spelling, a rockabilly tell)
- "Pin-up goes global"
- "Collaborators, creators & couples"

### Phrases to avoid
- Corporate / service-provider language ("packages," "services," "sessions start at")
- Distancing gallery-speak ("the artist," "the subject," "the work")
- Generic boudoir marketing ("indulge yourself," "reveal your beauty")

## Shoot themes — the book's category structure

| Book section | Sub-label | Web translation |
|---|---|---|
| My Pin-Up Story | Modern Self-Portraits | personal/about, not a portfolio category |
| Modern Pin-Up | Early Work 2005–2010 | archival — not a live category yet |
| Behind the Scenes | Modern Boudoir | matches "Pin-Up" on site, or its own category later |
| Pin-Up Fashion | Modern Glamour | matches "Vintage-Glamour" on site |
| Pin-Up Stylists | Modern Midcentury | overlaps "Tiki-Rockabilly" and "Vintage-Glamour" |
| Pin-Up Subcultures | Modern Kulture | not a current site category — interesting gap |
| Pin-Up Lifestyle | Modern Tiki | matches "Tiki-Rockabilly" partially |
| Pin-Up Goes Global | International Work | not a site category |
| Pin-Up Artists | Collaborators, Creators & Couples | burlesque performers, musicians, couples |

**Current site categories:** Pin-Up, Classic Cars, Burlesque, Tiki-Rockabilly, Vintage-Glamour.
**Book categories (simplified form):** Modern Pin-Up, Modern Glamour, Modern Midcentury, Modern Kulture, Modern Tiki, (International, Artists).

There's overlap but not alignment. The book's "Modern [X]" naming is more distinctive and more in-voice than the site's current neutral labels.

## The tension with the current site

| Dimension | Current site (Direction A "Gallery First") | The book |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Paper White #FAF9F6 | Saturated color blocks |
| Display type | Cormorant Garamond, light, italic | Script wordmark + bold italic serif |
| Accent color | Obsidian #1A1A1B only | Crimson, teal, rose, plum, blue, viridian |
| Voice of UI | Hushed, editorial, near-invisible | Confident, color-forward, clearly pin-up |
| Positioning | Fine-art photographer | Leading voice in modern pin-up |
| Borders | 1px obsidian thin lines | Full color blocks and frames |
| Motion | Subtle fades only | (irrelevant — print) |

**These are not the same brand.** Direction A positions Susana as a fine-art photographer. The book positions her as *the* contemporary voice in a living subculture. Susana sending us to the book as a brand reference strongly suggests she wants the site to move toward the book's energy — not all the way to a pink-and-script monograph on the web, but meaningfully toward it.

---

## Proposed moves (ordered by cost)

**Small — in-voice changes, same structure:**
1. Rename category labels using the "Modern [X]" convention on the drill-in headers (visitor sees "PIN-UP" on tiles but "Modern Pin-Up" when inside the category). Low-risk, high-signal.
2. Add the script "Vestige" wordmark somewhere in the header or footer, echoing the book's mark.
3. Introduce crimson (#C8142C or similar) as the single accent color for key interactive moments (Book a Session hover, active tile border, category label color).

**Medium — Direction B notes:**
4. Each category sub-page gets a thin colored bar or title block in its section color (Glamour = crimson, Midcentury = sage, Kulture = dusty blue, Tiki = viridian). Small splash of book palette without redesigning the page.
5. Pair a script word ("Susana" or the category name in script) with the italic serif category title on the drill-in header.

**Large — full direction shift:**
6. Rebuild the portfolio page on a colored section-tile model that mirrors the book's section openers: each category tile is a colored block with italic serif "Modern X" in the center, photos arranged around it as a collage.
7. Introduce the script + italic serif type pairing across the site, replacing the uniform Cormorant Garamond.

Recommend starting with moves 1–3. They inject her voice without discarding the restraint that makes the site feel premium.
