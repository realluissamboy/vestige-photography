# Voice Guide

PM uses this to match Luis's voice when drafting client-facing messages.

## Source

Extracted from Luis's IG DM thread with Susana (2026-04-17 and prior). Not a polished sample set — these are real messages. Update when Luis produces more formal client-facing examples for different client types.

## Observed patterns

- Lowercase when casual. "oh yes of course," "i just wanted a hero image," "oh you just read my mind! lol."
- Contractions always. "it's not a finished product," "I'll share an intake form."
- Short sentences, often two or three in a row rather than one compound sentence.
- Uses "lol" and emoji sparingly but genuinely.
- Direct about limitations without apologizing: "photography and modeling is way outside of what I'm accustomed to but I'm open to pushing myself."
- Names work state plainly: "still a lot of work to be done so it's not a finished product by any means."
- Invites rejection upfront: "let me know if you like it and love and keep going in that direction or hate it and want me to scrap it."
- Asks for input directly: "Please share any inspiration websites you have since it's helpful for me to align with you and your brand."
- Self-discloses when it fits: "I'm not normally a trusting guy but something told me to trust you."
- Never uses filler openers or closers.

## Real examples

### Example 1 — scoping pitch

> But hey, I can build a one-page minimalist website that contains a gallery of photos, a contact form or direct contact to your social media. It might take me some time since photography and modeling is way outside of what I'm accustomed to but I'm open to pushing myself. Please share any inspiration websites you have since it's helpful for me to align with you and your brand.

### Example 2 — review ask

> Your site is on the portfolio. There's still a lot of work to be done so it's not a finished product by any means. But let me know if you like it and love and keep going in that direction or hate it and want me to scrap it.

### Example 3 — context + next step

> Yup, like I mentioned, not anywhere near complete. Just want to know if it's going in the right direction with design and such. I'll share an intake form with you because I'm not familiar with the categories and want to get your "voice" which helps shape the text. Most of this is auto generated.

## Voice register rule for Susana

Close-friend-casual. Not agency-formal. Susana is an old friend first and a client second. Drafts to her should sound like Luis texting, not like Luis writing a proposal.

## Voice register rule for other clients (default)

Until Luis provides a separate sample set for cold or formal clients, default to a cleaner version of the above: contractions yes, lowercase no, no emoji, but still direct, short-sentenced, no filler.

## Hard rules (already enforced in PM)

- No "hope this finds you well"
- No "looking forward to connecting"
- No "please don't hesitate to reach out"
- No em dashes
- No: delve, leverage, seamlessly, robust, comprehensive, streamline, empower, utilize, unlock
